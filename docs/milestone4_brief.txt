Advanced Model Deployment and Findings
Having established baseline performance in Milestone 03, your team will now implement advanced machine learning
architectures (e.g., ensemble learning, regularized networks), perform rigorous hyperparameter optimization. This
milestone also requires you to conduct a thorough Algorithmic Bias and Fairness Audit to quantify algorithmic disparity
across sensitive attributes, evaluate accuracy-fairness trade-offs, and implement mitigation strategies (pre-processing,
in-processing, or post-processing) to ensure ethical deployment.

1. Required Technical & Analytical Deliverables
A. Baseline Model Implementation & Architecture Selection
 - Advanced Algorithm Implementation: Train at least two complex or ensemble architectures (e.g., Random Forests,
   Gradient Boosted Trees [XGBoost / LightGBM], Support Vector Machines, or Neural Networks) and benchmark them directly
   against your Milestone 03 baselines.
 - Systematic Hyperparameter Tuning: Execute a structured search (Grid Search, Random Search, or Bayesian Optimization)
   integrated with stratified K-Fold Cross-Validation to identify optimal model parameters without data leakage.
 - Comparative Performance Analysis: Evaluate improvement gains across primary metrics (Accuracy, Precision, Recall,
   F1-Score, ROC-AUC, and Precision-Recall AUC).
B. Algorithmic Bias and Fairness Audit
 - Sensitive Attribute Identification: Explicitly define the protected or sensitive attribute(s) in your dataset
   (e.g., gender, socio-economic class, geographic district, age bracket, or minority identity).
 - Quantitative Disparity Metrics: Compute key group fairness metrics across protected sub-populations:
     Demographic Parity / Disparate Impact Ratio: Ratio of positive prediction rates between unprivileged and privileged
     groups (evaluating compliance with the 80% rule).
     Equalized Odds / Equal Opportunity: Comparing True Positive Rates (TPR) and False Positive Rates (FPR) across groups
     to ensure equal error distribution.
     Predictive Equality: Comparing False Positive Rates to verify that non-failing / non-target cases are not
     disproportionately misclassified.
 - Fairness-Accuracy Trade-off Analysis: Map the Pareto frontier illustrating how enforcing fairness constraints impacts
   overall predictive accuracy.
C. Feature Importance & Initial Research Insights
 - FATEN Principles Alignment: Evaluate your finalized model against the FATEN framework: Fairness, Autonomy, Trust,
   Equity, and Non-maleficence.
 - Living Lab & Stakeholder Impact: Document how your algorithm would perform in a real-world living lab environment,
   identifying potential failure modes and governance safeguards needed to prevent digital marginalization.

2. Submission Requirements
 - Executable Code Notebook (.ipynb or .Rmd): Clean, fully documented, and reproducible code containing model training,
   cross-validation tuning grids, fairness metric calculations, bias mitigation steps, and threshold curves.
 - Manuscript Enhancement: Submitted draft towards the end of Milestone 03 further enhanced, detailing model
   architectures, hyperparameter search spaces, quantitative fairness audits, Pareto trade-off plots, and ethical
   governance recommendations.

3. Grading Rubric
 - Advanced Model Architecture & Optimization (30): Implementation of advanced/ensemble algorithms with systematic
   hyperparameter tuning via stratified CV. Clear comparative analysis against Milestone 03 baselines without data leakage.
 - Algorithmic Bias and Disparity Audit (35): Identification of sensitive attributes and thorough quantitative
   evaluation of group fairness metrics (Demographic Parity, Equalized Odds, Disparate Impact Ratio). Clear analysis of
   fairness vs. accuracy trade-offs.
 - Bias Mitigation & Asymmetric Threshold Tuning (20): Successful implementation of pre-, in-, or post-processing bias
   mitigation. Optimization of decision thresholds accounting for asymmetric error costs across demographic groups.
 - Written Report & Code Reproducibility (15): Fully executable, well-commented code notebook. High-quality PDF
   manuscript incorporating FATEN governance principles and living lab deployment considerations.
